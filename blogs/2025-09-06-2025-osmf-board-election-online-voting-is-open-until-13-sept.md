<p>Voting for the <a href="https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Foundation/AGM2025/Election_to_Board">2025 board election</a> has started. You can read the board candidates’ answers and manifestos <a href="https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Foundation/AGM2025/Election_to_Board/Answers_and_manifestos">here</a>.</p>



<p>Emails were sent to eligible OSM Foundation members today, and link directly to the voting page &#8211; listing each candidate in random order and allowing you to rank the candidates in order of preference.</p>



<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Eligibility to vote</h1>



<p>You are eligible to vote in the election if</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>you have been a member for the full 90 days prior to the date of the <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Annual_General_Meetings/2025">Annual General Meeting</a> <em>(which will take place on 13 September 2025)</em>, and</li>



<li>your membership is not in arrears 7 days before the date of the Annual General Meeting, and</li>



<li>you are a natural person.</li>
</ul>



<h1 class="wp-block-heading">OpaVote voting email</h1>



<p>If you are eligible to vote, the OpaVote email was sent to the email address associated with your OSM Foundation membership.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>The subject of the email is: [OpenStreetMap Foundation] Voting on 2025 board election [some_random_letters]</li>



<li>The sender is: noreply at opavote.com</li>
</ul>



<p>If you believe you should have received a voting email but can’t find it, please check your spam folder. If it’s still missing, send a message to the email addresses mentioned <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Annual_General_Meetings/2025/Voting_Information_and_Instructions#I_haven't_received_the_link_to_vote._Can_you_help?">here</a>.</p>



<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Voting information and FAQ</h1>



<p>You can find more <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Annual_General_Meetings/2025/Voting_Information_and_Instructions">information about voting and answers to frequently asked questions here</a>. Please make sure to read the warning on the page before voting.</p>



<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Deadline for voting: <a href="https://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?msg=OSMF+19th+Annual+General+Meeting+-+13th+September+2025&amp;iso=20250913T16&amp;p1=1440">Saturday 13 September 2025 at 16:00 UTC</a>,</h1>



<p>The polls will remain open for one week, closing on Saturday 13 September 2025 at 16:00 UTC, when the Annual General Meeting <em>(which you can join online)</em> will commence. We kindly ask you to vote in advance.</p>
