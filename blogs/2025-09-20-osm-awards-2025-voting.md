<p>The community voting for the OpenStreetMap Awards 2025 is open! During the call for nominees, you submitted more than a hundred of them. Then, a number of active community members have prepared a shorter list. Now it is again your turn: choose who gets an <a href="https://awards.openstreetmap.org/">award</a> during State of the Map in Manila.</p>
<p>We have 28 nominees to choose from 7 categories:</p>
<ul>
<li>Core Systems Award</li>
<li>Innovation Award</li>
<li>Influential Writing Award</li>
<li>Greatness in Mapping Award</li>
<li>Expanding the Community Award</li>
<li>Team Achievement Award</li>
<li>Ulf Möller Memorial Award</li>
</ul>
<p>Every one of these worked hard, and deserves every bit of our love. You don’t have to choose only one nominee per category: that would be an impossible choice, given we have so many great people on the list. Click as many checkboxes as you like, even all of them. What matters is not winning, but knowing that hundreds of people support you and your project. The main point of a community is to support its members, and this is your chance to do that. Don’t forget to press the “Vote” button afterwards.</p>
<p>And do use the fact that you can change or add to your votes at any time until the voting ends on <strong>30 September 2025</strong>, right before the State of the Map 2025 conference, at which winners will be announced.</p>
<p>Do not postpone visiting the website — spend a minute right now. Please vote now <a href="https://awards.openstreetmap.org/">awards.openstreetmap.org</a> and show your appreciation!</p>

The OpenStreetMap Foundation owns and maintains the infrastructure of the OpenStreetMap project, is financially supported by membership fees and&nbsp;<a href="https://donate.openstreetmap.org/">donations</a>, and organises the annual, international&nbsp;<a href="https://stateofthemap.org/">State of the Map</a> conference. <p><em><a href="https://www.openstreetmap.org/">OpenStreetMap</a> was founded in 2004 and is an international project to create a free map of the world. To do so, we, thousands of volunteers, collect data about roads, railways, rivers, forests, buildings and a lot more worldwide. Our map data can be downloaded for free by everyone and used for any purpose – including commercial usage. It is possible to produce your own maps which highlight certain features, to calculate routes etc. OpenStreetMap is increasingly used when one needs maps which can be very quickly, or easily, updated.</em></p>
