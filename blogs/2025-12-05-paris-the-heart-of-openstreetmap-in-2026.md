<p>Having hosted the Olympic Games, Paris is now preparing to welcome another international gathering centered on community, openness, and diversity. Like the sporting disciplines themselves, OpenStreetMap&#8217;s mapping covers a multitude of themes and territories. Nature and hiking enthusiasts will find a space for expression there, as well as professionals in the field of geographic data.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-text-align-center">Join us in Paris from August 28th to 30th, 2026, for State of the Map! <br /><br />Watch the <a href="https://peertube.openstreetmap.fr/w/kme7gzVKaqaK4tztBSkkWb">announcement video</a>.</p>



<p class="has-text-align-left">This annual gathering brings together OpenStreetMap volunteers, professionals, and enthusiasts to share knowledge, showcase innovations, and highlight the impact of open mapping.</p>



<p>Whether you&#8217;re a seasoned contributor, a curious newcomer, or a professional in the field, SotM 2026 is a chance for you to connect with the global community, learn about the latest developments, and contribute to the growth of OpenStreetMap. Mark your calendars and be part of this extraordinary event!</p>



<p>Save the date! Stay tuned via our website, <a href="https://2026.stateofthemap.org/">2026.stateofthemap.org</a> for more updates on speakers, agenda, ticketing, and how to get involved as the event draws closer.<br /></p>

<p><em>The <a href="https://stateofthemap.org/">State of the Map</a> conference is the annual, international conference of OpenStreetMap</em>, <em>organised by the <a href="https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/">OpenStreetMap Foundation</a>. The&nbsp;<a href="https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/">OpenStreetMap Foundation</a>&nbsp;is a not-for-profit organisation, formed to support the OpenStreetMap Project. It is dedicated to encouraging the growth, development and distribution of free geospatial data for anyone to use and share. The OpenStreetMap Foundation owns and maintains the infrastructure of the OpenStreetMap project, is financially supported by membership fees and&nbsp;<a href="https://donate.openstreetmap.org/">donations</a>, and organises the annual, international&nbsp;<a href="https://stateofthemap.org/">State of the Map</a> conference. Our volunteer <a href="https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/WorkingGroups">Working Groups</a> and small core staff work to support the OpenStreetMap project.&nbsp;<a href="https://join.osmfoundation.org/">Join the OpenStreetMap Foundation</a>&nbsp;for just £15 a year or <a href="https://supporting.openstreetmap.org/active-contributor-membership/">for free if you are an active OpenStreetMap contributor</a></em>.</p>



<p><em><a href="https://www.openstreetmap.org/">OpenStreetMap</a> was founded in 2004 and is an international project to create a free map of the world. Our map data can be downloaded for free by everyone and used for any purpose – including commercial usage.
