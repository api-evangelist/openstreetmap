<p>Whether you&#8217;re passionate about maps, data, or shaping the future of  OpenStreetMap (OSM), the community is always looking for your inspiring ideas! Why not sharing them during <a href="https://2026.stateofthemap.org/">State of the Map 2026</a>?</p>



<p>The <a href="https://2026.stateofthemap.org/calls/general/">call for participation</a> of SotM 2026, taking place in Paris, France, on <strong>August 28 &#8211; 30, 2026</strong>, is now open! The programme committee is ready and waiting, eager to unwrap your submissions for talks, workshops, and panels. These sessions aren&#8217;t just part of the conference; they&#8217;re its beating heart, driving conversations and sparking ideas that resonate worldwide. Presenting your work, projects and ideas at SotM is also a great way to get in touch with the wider OSM community.</p>



<p><strong>Tracks</strong></p>



<p>Sessions can be submitted for the following tracks:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>OSM Basics &#8211; Information dedicated to newcomers</li>



<li>Community and <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/">Foundation</a> &#8211; Bringing people together, <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Working_Groups">working group</a> experiences, strategies &amp; vision</li>



<li>Mapping &#8211; All about making the mapping easier and better</li>



<li>Cartography &#8211; Your ideas on how to create good-looking presentations of the OSM dataset</li>



<li>Software Development &#8211; Software for processing and editing data</li>



<li>Data Analysis &amp; Data Model &#8211; Reflections about the OSM data, its <a href="https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Elements">model</a> and analysis of quality and completeness</li>



<li>User Experiences &#8211; Stories of using OSM and its data as a user</li>



<li>Education &#8211; How you use OSM in an educational context</li>
</ul>



<p>If your submission doesn&#8217;t seem to fit into one of these tracks, don&#8217;t worry &#8211; as long as it is clearly related to OpenStreetMap, you&#8217;re perfectly fine if you simply choose the track that feels to fit best.</p>



<p><strong>OSM Science (Academic Track) at SotM 2026</strong></p>



<p>In addition to this general call for participation, there will again be a proper academic track with a separate CfP, which will be announced later. So, if you&#8217;re knee-deep in the captivating world of OpenStreetMap, stay tuned for the official call: The working group is eagerly awaiting the most riveting insights and groundbreaking results from your studies. Get your research hats on, gather your data, and prepare to submit the best of your studies.</p>



<p><strong>Timeline and Deadlines</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><a href="https://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?msg=End%20of%20State%20of%20the%20Map%202026%20Call%20for%20Participation&amp;iso=20260427T2359">27 April 2026 23:59:59 UTC</a>: Deadline talk, workshop and panel submissions</li>



<li><strong>End of May 2026</strong>: End of review phase, speakers will be informed, schedule published</li>



<li><strong>28-30 August 2026</strong>: State of the Map in Paris, France</li>
</ul>



<p>For more information on the above track categories, submission requirements and rating criteria, please visit the <a href="https://2026.stateofthemap.org/calls/general/">complete call for participation and the submission guidelines on the SotM website</a>.</p>



<p>Stay tuned for more news about the State of the Map 2026! See you later this year in Paris, France, and online!</p>
