The OpenStreetMap community can still submit poster presentations for State of the Map 2026; submissions must be A0 format, original, CC-BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 licensed, with generative AI allowed only for text refinement. The deadline is July 31, 2026, and up to 15 posters will be selected.
