OpenStreetMap Foundation is pleased and proud to announce that Proton AG has been recognised as a Platinum Corporate Member, following two donations over the past two years totalling around €70,000. Proton came to us with unsolicited donations both times. They believe in our project, the Foundation, but most importantly, our community.
